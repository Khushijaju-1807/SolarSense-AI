About SolarSense AI

SolarSense AI helps solar plant owners understand how their system performs and helps administrators keep every plant healthy.

It combines energy tracking, analytics and maintenance management with two explainable machine learning models: one predicts generation, the other detects abnormal drops.

The platform is built as a software-only system using simulated and historical data, and is designed so real IoT sensor data can be connected later.
